สวนคำ · The Word Garden — 180 Thai head words from the wichaa lexicon, each grown as a fractal tree. https://nanobotco.github.io/suan-kham/

A branch is a sense, growing out of the sense it extends (by metaphor, metonymy, narrowing, widening or euphemism). Each branch ends in a fractal with one leaf per compound filed under that sense. Weeds at the foot are lookalikes: words that only look like compounds of the head. The roots are the word's etymology (ancestor forms down the taproot, cognates in sister languages on the side roots) and its ThaiRoots family.

Sources: the wichaa lexicon's filing tables, entries and compound-filing notebook (Thai glosses from the Royal Institute dictionary and Wiktionary as the lexicon records them); the ThaiRoots inventory. English the lexicon lacks: en/drafted.json (DeepSeek V4 Flash) and en/session.json (Claude). Pictures: the motdang doodler (src/doodler.js, src/skyline.js) with src/doodler-kham-heads.js and src/doodler-kham-marks.js drawn for this app; src/tree.js draws the trees.

Build, with manuscript-wiki and thairoots checked out beside this folder (or SUANKHAM_LEXICON and SUANKHAM_THAIROOTS set): python3 tools/build_data.py && python3 tools/build.py. docs/ is the site.
Android: android/build.sh (see android/README.txt).
