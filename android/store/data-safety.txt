PLAY DATA SAFETY AND CONTENT ANSWERS — what the app does, for filling in the Play Console forms

What the app does with data, as built (version 1.0):
- It keeps two things in the WebView's own storage on the phone: the trail of words opened and the game score. Uninstalling the app removes them.
- It asks the phone's text-to-speech engine to read Thai words aloud when ฟัง is tapped.
- It makes one kind of outside request: when the phone is online, it asks Google Fonts (fonts.googleapis.com, fonts.gstatic.com) for typefaces. Links to other websites open in the phone's browser.
- Permission: INTERNET (for the typefaces).

Data safety form:
- Does your app collect or share any of the required user data types? No.
- Is all of the user data collected by your app encrypted in transit? Not applicable (no data collected). The font requests use HTTPS.
- Do you provide a way for users to request that their data is deleted? Not applicable; uninstalling removes the trail and score.
